## wine-atmosphere.mp4

Eight-second, 24 fps, 1280 × 720 H.264 silent loop authored locally from wine-editorial using a smooth cosine camera push-in/out. It is an animated still, not generative moving footage. The wine inside this video does not move independently. Real-time interactive wine movement is implemented separately in motion.js and the SVG glass.

Higgsfield text-to-video was attempted, but rejected with `Requires plus plan or higher`; no video generation job was created. Local Higgsedit was unavailable, so the animated-still clip was encoded with PyAV/H.264. No subscription was purchased.

The WebP images and MP4 are stored with the website, and do not depend on external hosting.
